Install Track Anything

Add Track Anything to your Home Screen or install it for convenient access. It opens in its own window.

If this page is open inside another app, that app’s menu may have a way to open it in your browser.

  1. Open the browser’s Share menu {{icon "share-up"}}. In Safari, Share may be inside the Page Menu.
  2. Choose “Add to Home Screen”. You may need to scroll through the actions.
  3. If “Open as Web App” appears, leave it enabled.
  4. Tap “Add”.
